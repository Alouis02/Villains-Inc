Take your superhero gameplay to the next level with Superhuman Flying Locomotion! This pack features 51 high-quality flying animations, covering everything from normal flight to speed flight and boost flight.  Whether your character is soaring through the skies, dodging attacks,  or making sharp turns, this pack provides smooth and natural flying  transitions. All animations are crafted in Autodesk Maya and are compatible with Unreal Engine. You can easily modify flight directions and transitions to suit your project needs. This is a continuation of my previous flying animation packs. A custom skeleton rig (not compatible with Unity Humanoid)
